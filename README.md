🔍 Exploratory Data Analysis & Insights1. Cancellation Distribution62.87% (74,745 bookings) of guests completed their stay (Checked Out).37.13% (44,152 bookings) canceled or registered as no-shows.Takeaway: Cancellation is an institutional operational reality requiring proactive hedging rather than passive management.2. City Hotel vs. Resort HotelCity Hotels register an alarmingly high cancellation rate of 41.71% across 79,330 reservations.Resort Hotels maintain a lower cancellation rate of 27.98% across 39,567 reservations.Takeaway: Business travelers adjust travel schedules on short notice, whereas leisure guests coordinate complex travel logistics (flights, itineraries), creating stickier commitment.3. Average Daily Rate (ADR) CorrelationTime-series plotting demonstrates a strong positive correlation between surges in daily room tariffs and elevated cancellation ratios.Takeaway: Aggressive peak-rate surges lead customers to lock in reservations speculatively, only to cancel later when competitor rates soften or discount promotions appear.4. Lead Time & Booking BehaviorAn average lead time of 104.3 days (reaching up to 737 days) leaves excessive time for travelers' plans to change.The overwhelming majority of reservations belong to Transient guests booking via Online Travel Agencies (OTAs) with "No Deposit" policies, resulting in zero financial consequence for canceling.💡 Strategic Business SuggestionsImplement a Tiered Deposit StructureFor reservations made 45+ days in advance, introduce a non-refundable partial deposit (10–20%).Establish dual pricing: a discounted, strictly non-refundable tier alongside a standard flexible tier.Tailored Retention for City HotelsContract corporate partners and negotiated enterprise rates to offset the 41.71% churn from individual transient travelers.Tighten the penalty-free cancellation window from open-ended terms to a strict 48–72 hours prior to arrival.Dynamic Value-Add Bundling Over Price SurgesInstead of imposing standalone tariff hikes during peak demand periods, bundle room rates with high-perceived-value inclusions (complimentary breakfast, airport transfers, dining credits). This deters guests from comparative price-shopping.Automated Milestone EngagementDeploy automated touchpoints at 14 days, 7 days, and 3 days prior to check-in via SMS/WhatsApp/email.Requesting arrival times or room preferences establishes psychological commitment and reveals cancellation intent early.Incentivize Direct Website BookingsMitigate over-reliance on third-party OTAs by offering exclusive direct-booking benefits (free room upgrades, late checkout, loyalty points). Direct bookers historically display significantly lower cancellation rates.Promote Credit Vouchers Over Cash RefundsWhen a guest initiates a cancellation, provide an alternative of a 110% hotel credit voucher redeemable for future dates, safeguarding immediate operating liquidity.📂 Project Architecture├── data/
